# Track 4 — Upland + ATLAS:EARTH + Off-World Ventures

## Purpose

Extend the multiverse property system into fictional entertainment/IP layers for planets, stations, galaxies, and time-route districts.

## Important Guardrail

Off-world assets in this track are creative, fictional, worldbuilding, collectible, educational, and entertainment-oriented concepts.

They are not claims of literal ownership of real astronomical bodies.

## Business Lines

### Off-World Realtor Bot
- help users choose fictional planets, districts, or colonies
- create themed brochures and charter narratives
- map business sectors and colony identities

### Planet Builder Studio
- create world profiles
- define economy, districts, factions, and lore
- generate launch pages, posters, and narratives

### Galactic Trade & District Systems
- fictional ports, routes, and interdimensional corridors
- branded logistics and commerce concepts
- universe-scale district and franchise planning

## Shared Model

- world_name
- world_type
- faction_or_theme
- economy_model
- district_list
- lore_summary
- business_use_case

## MVP

1. off-world asset generator
2. planet profile templates

4. fictional deed/charter generator
5. franchise catalog page
