# Bot Roles

## 1. Property Fleet Bot
Scores and classifies holdings.

## 2. RoboDecor Bot
Designs district themes, decor ideas, and marketing copy.

## 3. Upland Realtor Robot
Guides buying, selling, and portfolio packaging in Upland.

## 4. Atlas Expansion Bot
Plans parcel concentration and city/territory growth in ATLAS:EARTH.

## 5. Multiverse Property Brain
Unifies recommendations across all tracks.

## 6. Off-World Realtor Bot
Generates fictional planetary and galactic real-estate experiences.

## 7. Planet Builder Studio Bot
Builds worlds, districts, factions, and launch narratives.

## 8. Marketing & Media Bot
Creates landing page copy, ads, lore packs, launch posts, and educational content.
