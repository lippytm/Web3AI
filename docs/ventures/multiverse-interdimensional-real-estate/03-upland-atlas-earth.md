# Track 3 — Upland + ATLAS:EARTH

## Purpose

Unify city-based virtual property strategy with world-mapped territorial strategy into one AI-managed multiverse portfolio system.

## Business Lines

### Multiverse Property Brain
- compare Upland and ATLAS:EARTH opportunities
- recommend where the next dollar of effort should go
- score clustering, utility, identity, and expansion potential

### Multiverse Realtor Concierge
- ask goals, budget, and style
- route users into Upland, ATLAS:EARTH, or both
- create step-by-step plans and portfolio narratives

## Shared Model

- platform
- region_or_city
- holding_count
- concentration_score
- identity_theme
- yield_or_value_notes
- next_action

## MVP

1. unified holdings schema
2. cross-platform scoring engine
3. recommendation dashboard
4. buyer/empire planning chatbot
5. monthly strategy report generator

## Revenue Ideas

- portfolio intelligence subscription
- strategy calls and reports
- AI concierge for players and communities
- cross-platform empire planning packages
