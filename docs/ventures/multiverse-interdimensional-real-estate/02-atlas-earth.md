# Track 2 — ATLAS:EARTH

## Purpose

Build AI-powered strategy systems around parcel acquisition, territory concentration, travel logic, merchant reward routes, and leadership-oriented expansion planning.

## Business Lines

### Atlas Expansion Bot
- rank cities and territories
- suggest concentrate vs diversify decisions
- align acquisitions with travel and spending habits
- support city leadership playbooks

### Atlas Territory Strategy Lab
- city conquest plans
- local route and merchant planning
- territory heat maps and watchlists
- growth reports

## Data Model

- city
- state_or_region
- parcel_count
- target_title
- travel_patterns
- merchant_patterns
- strategic_role
- notes

## MVP

1. parcel/territory intake form
2. concentration scoring model
3. city priority recommendations
4. city-by-city dashboard
5. travel-linked action planner

## Revenue Ideas

- territory strategy reviews
- city domination reports
- route planning services
- guided onboarding for new users
